I am a Ph.D. candidate in machine learning at the H. Milton Stewart School of Industrial and Systems Engineering at the Georgia Institute of Technology, advised by Professor Yao Xie.

I earned my M.Sc. in Computational and Applied Mathematics from the University of Chicago and my B.Sc. in Statistics from Peking University. My research interests include machine learning, diffusion large language models, uncertainty quantification, and reinforcement learning.
